## 3. Campaign Architecture & Funnel Strategy
### A. Instant Forms (Native Lead Gen) with High Intent Filters
- Using conditional logic and custom qualifying questions (e.g., "Are you looking to buy in 0–3 months or 3–6 months?", "Expected budget bracket").
- Why default autofill produces low-quality leads and how custom friction questions filter out casual scrollers.

### B. Click-to-WhatsApp Ads
- Direct conversational routing to property consultants for immediate engagement.
- Automated pre-filled WhatsApp conversation starters with project brochure triggers.

### C. Landing Page Funnels
- When dedicated landing pages outperform instant forms (high-ticket projects requiring video virtual tours and interactive floorplans).

---

## 4. Creative Strategy: What Resonates With Kerala Buyers
- **Drone & Construction Walkthroughs:** Authentic on-site progress videos out-convert 3D renders.
- **Architect / Developer Founder Spotlights:** Building founder authority and structural transparency.
- **Location Advantage Visuals:** Proximity to key transit hubs, schools, and hospitals mapped clearly.
- **Malayalam vs. English Copy Split Testing:** Tailoring regional language hooks for Tier-2 districts and clean English for cosmopolitan NRI demographics.

---

## 5. Targeting Framework: Domestic vs. GCC NRIs
- Domestic targeting: Geo-radius around project site + affinity interest layering (investment, property, luxury goods).
- GCC NRI targeting: Kerala diaspora targeting in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Doha, Riyadh, and Jeddah using language, cultural interest signals, and frequent international traveler profiles.

---

## 6. Lead Nurturing & Preventing Lead Decay
- Lead half-life: Response within 5–10 minutes yields a 4x higher contact rate.
- Automated CRM and WhatsApp webhook dispatch workflows.
