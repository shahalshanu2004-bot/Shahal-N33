## 1. Intent vs. Discovery: The Fundamental Behavioral Divide
- **Google Search:** High-intent pull marketing. Users active in the problem-solving state searching for immediate vendors.
- **Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram):** High-impact push discovery. Creating demand by identifying prospective buyers matching precise demographics and behavioral interests.

## 3. When to Choose Google Ads First
- The service solves an immediate, painful emergency (e.g., commercial maintenance, legal disputes, medical clinics).
- Clear, established search volume exists with commercial intent keywords.
- Customer consideration cycle is short (decision made within 24 to 48 hours).
- High competitive margins justify paying ₹50–₹200+ per click.

---

## 4. When to Choose Meta Ads First
- The offer is visually demonstrable or benefits from video storytelling (real estate, interior design, coaching, SaaS demo).
- You need high lead volume to feed an active outbound or inside sales team.
- You want to qualify leads conversational through direct Click-to-WhatsApp routing.
- The product is novel and lacks existing search demand.

---

## 5. The Hybrid Growth Architecture: The Best of Both Worlds
- **Step 1:** Capture existing high-intent demand on Google Search with strict exact/phrase match negative keyword lists.
- **Step 2:** Retarget non-converting Google visitors on Meta with proof testimonials, case studies, and founder videos.
- **Step 3:** Use Meta lookalikes of closed customers to generate top-of-funnel demand at 60% lower media costs.
